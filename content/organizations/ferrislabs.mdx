## Maintainer notes

This entry was written from FerrisLabs' public repositories rather than
submitted by them, and nobody there has confirmed it. The evidence is ADR 0005,
"Apache Iggy carries actions and outcomes inside a data plane" (status
proposed, dated 2026-10-20), the libs/autharie-iggy crate and the Iggy Helm
template in [ferrislabs/autharie](https://github.com/ferrislabs/autharie), and
the libs/bus-iggy crate used by the API and worker in
[ferrislabs/capitainerie](https://github.com/ferrislabs/capitainerie), read
2026-10-22.

The logo is taken from the organisation's own public materials: the mark
served on ferrislabs.fr (/logo.svg).
