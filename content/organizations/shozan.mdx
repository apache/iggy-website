## Maintainer notes

This entry was written from Shozan's public repository rather than submitted
by them, and nobody there has confirmed it. The evidence is
[ShozanHQ/mansio](https://github.com/ShozanHQ/mansio), read 2026-10-29: its
README says "Apache Iggy is the broker, and mansio is the contract around it",
the mansio-iggy crate implements the broker, and the compose file starts Iggy
0.9.0.

The logo is taken from the organisation's own public materials: the icon served
on shozan.ai (/icon.svg).
