## Maintainer notes

This entry was written from Songbird's public repository rather than submitted
by them, and nobody there has confirmed it. The evidence is the `iggy` 0.12.0
dependency in the workspace Cargo.toml, Iggy 0.10.0 in the compose stack, and
decisions D-0029 ("the mailbox store is Apache Iggy") and D-0030 in
docs/DECISIONS.md of
[songbirdchat/songbird](https://github.com/songbirdchat/songbird), read
2026-11-20. The backend is still being built and no apps are released.

The website is left out rather than guessed: neither the organisation nor the
repository gives one, and songbird.chat, named in the design notes, returned
404. No logo: the brand notes say the mark is not drawn yet, and the GitHub
avatar is a generated identicon.
