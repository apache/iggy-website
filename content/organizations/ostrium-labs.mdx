## Maintainer notes

This entry was written from Ostrium Labs' public repositories rather than
submitted by them, and nobody there has confirmed it. The evidence is the
crates/loams-iggy-sink crate and the `kafka` compose profile in
[ostrium-labs/loams](https://github.com/ostrium-labs/loams), and their fork
[ostrium-labs/iggy](https://github.com/ostrium-labs/iggy), which builds Kafka
wire compatibility in the gateway for Loams, read 2026-10-29. On
apache/iggy#3560 (2026-10-21) Dinakaran from Loams wrote "We've chosen Iggy as
our event-ingest log".

The logo is taken from the organisation's own public materials: the mark served
on ostriumlabs.org (/favicon.svg), in its light and dark colours.
