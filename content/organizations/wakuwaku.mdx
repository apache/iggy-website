## Maintainer notes

This entry was written from wakuwaku's public repository rather than submitted
by them, and nobody there has confirmed it. The evidence is the wakuwaku-iggy
crate and the Iggy consumer registration in
[suitsu31-club/wakuwaku](https://github.com/suitsu31-club/wakuwaku), read
2026-10-15. Until September the framework used an AMQP consumer tested against
RabbitMQ; from 1.0.0-rc1 on 2026-10-11 its messaging is Iggy. The website lists
"Typed events on Apache Iggy" as a feature.

The logo is taken from the organisation's own public materials: the icon served
on wakuwaku.rs (/icon.svg).
